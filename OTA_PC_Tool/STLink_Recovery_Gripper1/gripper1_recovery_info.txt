STM32G431夹爪ST-Link救援包
Role: 1
Version: 1.15.0
Application: 0x08006000, 46968 bytes, CRC32=0xAEEE2162
Bootloader: 0x08000000, 22536 bytes
Metadata: 0x0801F000, 2048 bytes
Calibration excluded: 0x0801F800~0x0801FFFF

普通Application救援使用*_stlink_app_recovery.hex；
